About Indu

Gratitude begins the moment we pause long enough to notice what's already here

I'm so grateful you're here. In a world that constantly asks us to do more, achieve more, and become more, it's easy to forget to pause and appreciate what already exists in our lives.

As a life coach based in Gurgaon, I work with people who are ready to stop moving on autopilot — to notice their patterns, question the stories they've been carrying, and choose, with intention, who they want to become. My approach blends structured reflection with genuine warmth: there's no perfect way to do this work, only an honest one.

Whether you're picking up the free Gratitude Journal or joining me for a full coaching journey, I'm walking beside you — one grateful, honest step at a time.

The Way Back To You

A six-session transformation journey

Level 2 of the coaching path — six core sessions, two integration sessions, and your Gratitude Journal, designed to move you from clarity to intentional action.

1

The Moment You Take Your Power Back

From feeling like life is happening to me, to recognising the choices I have within it.

We shift from "why is this happening to me?" to "what can I choose from here?" — building self-awareness and clarity around who you are and where you want to go.

2

The Stories You Were Never Meant To Believe

From being controlled by old stories, to consciously choosing what you want to believe.

We recognise the beliefs quietly shaping your choices and feelings, and begin separating what's true from what you've simply learned to believe.

3

What Do You Really Want?

From moving through life on autopilot, to consciously choosing where you want to go.

We create space to step back from expectations and get clear about the direction you actually want your life to take.

4

The Enemy Within

From fighting your resistance, to understanding it and choosing how you respond.

We look at how resistance shows up — procrastination, overthinking, perfectionism, avoidance — and what it may be trying to protect you from.

5

The Voice You Live With

From unconsciously repeating an old story, to consciously choosing a new one.

We bring awareness to your inner voice and the beliefs behind it, and work on consciously creating a more empowering one.

6

See It. Believe It. Become It.

From imagining a different life, to taking action toward creating it.

We bring the journey together through visualisation and action — moving you from knowing what you want to actually doing something about it.
